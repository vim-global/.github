# Vim Global

## Digital Habitat Infrastructure

Modular infrastructure for branded digital environments that connect people, businesses, content, commerce, and analytics.

[Visit vim.global](https://vim.global) · [Contact our team](mailto:office@vim.global)

## About Vim Global

Vim Global develops **Digital Habitats**: connected digital environments shaped around a brand, community, person, or pet. A Habitat brings digital identity, communication, content, commercial experiences, and analytics into one coherent space.

Its owner defines participation rules, roles, access, content, and offers within the platform’s capabilities and the shared Vim Global rules. Instead of treating every link, profile, and interaction as a separate destination, a Habitat connects them around a clear purpose.

**Development stage:** the integrated platform is in MVP development. Product and module availability varies; we confirm the current scope of each solution before delivery. The ecosystem below describes our direction, not a claim that every capability is already launched.

## Our Platform

| Module | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Domain** | Public entry to a branded environment, with presentation and onboarding. |
| **Connect** | Participants, roles, access, communication, and environment management. |
| **Flow** | Managed links, QR entry points, and action analytics. |
| **Store** | Commerce and marketplace scenarios when connected to a Habitat. |
| **Grow** | Learning experiences within a Habitat. |
| **Gate** | Events, registration, tickets, and passes. |

**Hub** is the branded space within a digital product that connects its content and actions. It is a product component, rather than an additional platform module.

## Our Products

### Digital Identity

- **Presence** — the bundle of Self, Key, and BioPage that connects a person’s digital presence to a Habitat.
- **Self** — a digital profile for a person or brand, bringing information, contacts, links, and actions together.
- **Key** — a personal Wallet pass whose QR opens the linked Self.
- **BioPage** — an independently managed link-in-bio page for content, contacts, and offers.

### Business Solutions

**Channel** extends a good, object, or service into a direct digital brand channel. **Channel Timer** is an extended edition for follow-up actions such as service, renewal, or repeat orders.

### Physical & Connected Products

**Self cards and badges, optional Paw tags, and Video cards** connect physical touchpoints to digital experiences through QR, NFC, or URL, depending on the item.

**Paw** is a digital product for a pet; a physical tag is optional. **Video** is a personal digital video-message page. Physical items support these experiences and remain distinct from the digital products. Availability is confirmed for each item and configuration.
